# Disney+ Hotstar Content Analysis

## Project Overview

This project involves comprehensive data analysis and visualization of Disney+ Hotstar's content catalog. Using Excel and Power BI, we explored key metrics such as genre distribution, content type trends, release timelines, age ratings, and audience targeting. The project aims to generate strategic insights that can guide content strategy, improve audience engagement, and refine marketing and production planning.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze content distribution by genre, release year, running time, and age ratings.
- Identify popular and emerging genres across movies and TV shows.
- Segment content based on running time and audience age groups.
- Detect trends in production and genre evolution to inform future releases.
- Support marketing and production strategies with data-driven insights.

---

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains metadata about Disney+ Hotstar content, including:

- `hotstar_id`
- `title`
- `description`
- `genre`
- `year`
- `age_rating`
- `running_time`
- `seasons`
- `episodes`
- `type` (Movie or TV)

## Key Insights

### **Genre Trends**
- **Drama** dominates movie content; **Reality** leads TV genres
- **Comedy**, **Romance**, and **Action** are consistently popular
- Balanced representation for **Thriller** and **Family** genres across types

### **Running Time**
- Longest: **Sport**, **Biopic**, and **Action**
- Shortest: **Shorts**, **Science**, **Travel**
- Most movies are 90–150 minutes; post-2000, runtime variation increased

### **Trends in Content Releases**
- Movie production spiked post-2000 with the rise of digital streaming
- TV content growth surged after 2012, peaking in 2021
- Slight decline after 2022 possibly due to market saturation

### **Age Ratings**
- **U/A 13+** dominates (43%), ideal for teen & family targeting
- **A-rated** (5%) indicates room for more adult-only content

### **Movie vs TV Content**
- Movies are twice as numerous as TV shows
- TV shows have longer production cycles and deeper audience engagement

---

## Strategic Recommendations

### **Content Strategy**
- Prioritize **Drama for Movies** and **Reality for TV**
- Invest in binge-worthy **TV series and mini-series**
- Expand **A-rated** content for adult audience capture

### **Growth & Monetization**
- Promote **long-form content** (e.g., sports, biopics) through extended versions
- Develop **short-form content hubs** to cater to casual viewers

### **Release Planning**
- Schedule releases around peak seasons (e.g., holidays, events)
- Maintain regular release cadence to match post-2000 trends

### **Marketing & Engagement**
- Run genre-specific campaigns for **Action**, **Drama**, and **Comedy**
- Use **U/A 13+** content for wide reach campaigns
